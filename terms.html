Terms & Conditions

Everything you need to know about renting with Élan, from reservations and payments to dress care, returns, and customer responsibilities.

1. Rental requests

Submitting a rental request does not guarantee dress availability or create a completed paid reservation. Submitting the checkout form saves a request only in your browser. Contact ÉLAN directly to confirm availability and complete your reservation.

2. Rental period

Select a rental period of one to four calendar days, including the pickup/start and return dates. Dress prices displayed in the catalog are the listed rental prices for that period.

3. Dress care and returns

Customers should handle dresses carefully and return them on the agreed date. Any deposits, late fees, damage charges, or cleaning policies must be disclosed and agreed to separately before real rentals begin.

4. Payments

Payment options indicate your preference only. Online payments are not processed through this website, and no card information is collected.

5. Membership

Membership inquiries are expressions of interest only and do not activate a subscription or charge a membership fee.

6. Questions

For questions, visit our Contact Us page.
