FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Find answers to common questions about rental, booking, payments, delivery, and more. If you need further assistance, feel free to contact us anytime.

How does the dress rental process work?

Browse our curated collection, choose your preferred dress, select your rental dates, complete the booking and payment, and we'll deliver the dress to your doorstep.

After your rental period, simply return the dress using the provided return instructions.

How long is the rental period?

Our standard rental period is 4 days: 1 day for delivery, 2 days for your event, and 1 day for return.

You can select your preferred rental dates during checkout. An extended rental period may be available for an additional fee.

What sizes are available?

Our dresses are available in sizes XS, S, M, and L. Each product page includes a detailed size guide to help you find the best fit.

If you're unsure about your size, you can check the specific measurements listed for each dress.

Can I extend my rental period?

Yes, you can extend your rental period subject to availability.

Additional fees may apply. Please contact us as early as possible if you need to extend your reservation.

What happens if I damage the dress?

Minor signs of wear from normal use are expected. However, if the dress is severely damaged, torn, stained, or otherwise beyond normal cleaning, additional fees may apply.

We will assess the damage after the dress is returned and inform you of any charges.

Do you offer delivery outside NCR?

Yes, we deliver to select areas outside Metro Manila. Delivery fees and turnaround times may vary depending on your location.

Please check the available delivery options at checkout or contact us for more details.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel your booking, but please do so at least 3 days before your delivery date to receive a full refund.

Cancellations made less than 3 days before delivery date may be subject to a cancellation fee.

How do I return the dress?

After your rental period, place the dress back in the provided garment bag, secure it in the original packaging, and use the prepaid return label.

You can drop it off at the nearest courier partner or schedule a pickup depending on your location.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are processed for eligible cancellations and in cases where you cannot fulfill your booking.

Refunds are not provided for changes of mind after delivery or for minor wear incurred during the rental period.

Is there a membership commitment?

No, there is no long-term commitment.

You can choose any plan and cancel when you need to. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your membership anytime.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfers.

All payments are securely processed through our trusted payment partners.

Can I walk in and rent a dress directly?

We currently operate by online booking only to ensure availability and a smooth experience for our customers.

Walk-ins may not always be accommodated, so please contact us in advance to check availability.
