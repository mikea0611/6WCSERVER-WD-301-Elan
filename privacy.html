Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Élan collects, uses, and protects your personal information to ensure a safe and secure rental experience.

1. Information you provide

The checkout form requests your name, email, Philippine mobile number, address, rental dates, and a payment preference. The membership inquiry form requests contact details and your chosen plan.

2. How information is stored

Rental requests and shopping bag information are stored in your browser's localStorage. Checkout details are not transmitted to a server or payment processor. Avoid entering sensitive personal information while testing.

3. Why information is collected

The checkout fields help you prepare a rental request, and membership inquiry fields collect your preferences locally. Customer records displayed on the separate technical data page are fictional examples.

4. Your choices

You can clear this website's saved browser data using your browser settings. For a real launch, ÉLAN would need a secure backend, retention policy, and a process for privacy requests.

5. Contact

Questions? Visit our Contact Us page.
